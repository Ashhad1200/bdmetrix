# BD Matrix — Website Fix Plan (V1)

> Working document. Read top-to-bottom once. Then execute section-by-section.

---

## 0. The Core Diagnosis (Read This First)

Your website is not failing because of design. It's failing because of **trust**. Right now BD Matrix reads like:

- A small Karachi team pretending to be a global agency
- Generic agency language anyone could have written
- Inflated stats (50+ projects, 98% retention, +41% revenue improvement)
- Stock-feeling testimonials from "Toronto, Vancouver, London"
- 8 different services — meaning no specialty

Sophisticated buyers (the ones who actually pay) read this and quietly leave. They don't tell you why. They just ghost on WhatsApp. That's exactly what's happening.

**The fix is not to polish what's there. The fix is to strip it down, get honest, and pick a lane.**

The new BD Matrix should be:
- **Specific** (one or two services, one vertical)
- **Honest** (real claims, real clients, real receipts)
- **Local-first** (Pakistan-based, proud of it, with global capability)
- **Founder-led** (Ashhad is the face — like Afnan Devs works)

- "We design and build software that improves revenue, efficiency, and operational performance" → too broad

**ADD (new copy):**

>

>

## 3. NEW SECTIONS TO ADD

### 3.1 Pricing or "How We Charge"

Most agency sites hide pricing. That's exactly why buyers ghost — they assume you're expensive or shady. Be different.

Add a "Pricing" or "Investment" section:

> **Our POS system starts at PKR [X] for single-outlet setup, and PKR [Y] per additional outlet. Includes 6 months of support. Custom integrations and AI features are quoted separately.**
>
> [Book a demo for a precise quote →]

Even a price range filters out tire-kickers and pulls in real buyers. You'll get fewer leads, but every lead will be 10x more serious.

### 3.2 A Real Case Study Page (At Least One)

One full case study — long, detailed, with real screenshots — beats 10 marketing taglines.

Structure:
- Client name and background
- The problem they had before
- What you built
- Screenshots / video walkthrough
- Results (real numbers — even small ones)
- Quote from the client

This single page will close more deals than the rest of the website combined.

### 3.3 Demo Booking Page

Replace "30-minute complimentary consultation" with something specific:

> **Book a 15-minute POS demo**
>
> See exactly how the system works for your type of business. We'll walk through a real setup on a real screen, answer your questions, and tell you if we're the right fit. No pitch deck. No sales pressure. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you and recommend someone who is.
>
> [Pick a time →]

The "we'll tell you if we're not the right fit" line builds enormous trust.

## 6. WHAT COMES AFTER THE WEBSITE

The website is necessary but not sufficient. Once it's fixed, these are next:

1. **Rewrite the Meta ad creative** to match the new positioning. Stop running broad "we build software" ads. Start running specific "POS for Karachi restaurants" ads with video walkthroughs of the actual product.

2. **Fix the WhatsApp first message.** Voice note, within 5 minutes, by name, with one specific question. (We discussed this in chat — implement it.)

3. **Build a real lead qualification step.** Before any lead gets a WhatsApp message, they should answer 2 questions on the form: "How many outlets?" and "When are you looking to deploy?" This filters tire-kickers.

4. **Start a content engine.** Even one short LinkedIn post per week from Ashhad's personal account, talking about real things you've built. Founder content > agency content, every time.

5. **Get the first 3 real wins documented as case studies.** Each case study becomes a new ad, a new social post, a new piece of trust.

## 7. FINAL PRINCIPLE

When in doubt, ask: *"Would I believe this if I read it on a competitor's website?"*

If the answer is no — cut it. Honest and small closes way more than inflated and big. Every credible service business in Pakistan that scales past 7-figure revenue did it by being undeniably specific about one thing, with verifiable proof.

That's the move. Start there.
